論理の塔 - LOGIC TOWER

Support

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This English page is a reference translation of the Japanese original, provided for convenience. The Japanese text is the official version; where the two differ, the Japanese text applies.

“論理の塔 - LOGIC TOWER” is a smartphone puzzle game in which you advance through a tower by solving short logic quizzes.

How to Play

  • In Floor Challenge (フロア攻略), you take on the logic quizzes of the floor you select.
  • In Tower Challenge (塔への挑戦), you try to solve as many questions as you can within 60 seconds.
  • Collecting Fragments of Wisdom (知恵の欠片) lets you unlock additional questions from the Sealed Tablets (封印の石板).
  • BGM, sound effects and vibration can be changed on the Tuning (調律) screen.

FAQ

Do I need to register an account?

No. There is no account creation or login feature.

Where is game data stored?

Progress and settings are stored on your device. If you delete the app, the saved data may be deleted as well.

Are there ads?

The only ads are rewarded ads, shown only when you choose to watch them. When you finish watching an ad, one “Lantern” (灯火), the in-game life, is restored. Even if you do not watch ads, Lanterns recover naturally over time.
