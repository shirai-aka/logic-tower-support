論理の塔 - LOGIC TOWER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This English page is a reference translation of the Japanese original, provided for convenience. The Japanese text is the official version; where the two differ, the Japanese text applies.

“論理の塔 - LOGIC TOWER” (the “App”) is a smartphone puzzle game in which you advance through a tower by solving logic quizzes. This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

1. Information the App Obtains Directly

The App does not ask you to enter, and does not obtain, information that directly identifies you personally, such as your name, address, telephone number or email address.

To provide the game experience, the App may store the following information on your device.

  • Game progress
  • Questions answered correctly and question history
  • In-game state such as Lanterns (灯火) and Fragments of Wisdom (知恵の欠片)
  • Settings such as volume, BGM, sound effects and vibration
  • Records such as your personal best in the time attack

As a rule, this information is stored on your device and is not obtained or managed by the developer on an external server.

2. Purposes of Use

Information handled by the App is used for the following purposes.

  • Saving game progress
  • Managing which questions are presented
  • Saving settings such as volume
  • Displaying ads
  • Granting in-game rewards when an ad has been watched to completion
  • Improving the quality of the App and fixing defects

3. Provision to Third Parties

Except where required by law, the App does not sell or provide information that directly identifies you personally to third parties.

However, where an ad delivery service is used, the ad service provider may obtain information necessary for delivering ads.

4. Third-Party SDKs and Analytics Tools

The App uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to deliver ads.

Ad requests are marked as requests for non-personalized ads, and the content rating of the ads shown is limited to the equivalent of “General”.

At present, the App does not include any external analytics tool of its own.

If an analytics tool for crash analysis, usage analysis or quality improvement is introduced in the future, this policy will be updated to state the information obtained, the purposes of use and whether it is provided to third parties.

5. Advertising

The App shows rewarded ads, which are watched only when you voluntarily choose to do so.

When you finish watching an ad, one “Lantern” (灯火), the in-game life, is restored. Even if you do not watch ads, Lanterns recover naturally over time.

The App does not use the following ad formats.

  • Ads that are shown forcibly
  • Ads that are shown automatically after game results
  • Banner ads that are displayed at all times

Ad services such as Google AdMob may obtain device information, advertising identifiers, ad usage, ad display history, in-app interaction information, diagnostic information, performance information and similar data for purposes such as displaying ads, measuring ad effectiveness, preventing fraud and optimizing ad delivery.

For how Google handles information, please see the following.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob

6. App Tracking Transparency

The current iOS version is not designed around ad delivery that uses the advertising identifier (IDFA), and it does not show the permission dialog under Apple’s App Tracking Transparency.

If ad delivery is changed in the future to use the IDFA, we will ask for your permission as required and update this policy and the privacy information on the App Store.

7. Device Permissions

The App does not request permissions such as location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. If a new permission becomes necessary in a future version, this policy will be updated and the change announced on this page.

8. Data Management and Deletion

The App’s progress and settings are stored mainly on your device. If you uninstall the App, the game data stored on your device may be deleted as well.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information such as names or email addresses from children under 13.

10. Revisions

This policy may be revised in response to new App features, the introduction of third-party SDKs, or changes in laws or platform rules. Revisions will be published on this page.
